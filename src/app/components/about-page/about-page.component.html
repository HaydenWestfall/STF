About STF

An independent agency built on relationships

Spieles Troutwine Fout Insurance Group has protected families, farms and businesses across west-central Ohio for more than 60 years.

Who we are

Local people, national markets

STF Insurance Group is an independent insurance agency headquartered in Arcanum, Ohio, with additional offices in Vandalia and Middletown. As an independent agency we are not tied to a single insurance company — instead we hold contracts with more than 20 regional and national carriers and place each client with the one that fits them best.

That structure matters most when something changes. A new roof, a teenage driver, a business that outgrows its policy, a rate increase, or a claim on your record can all make yesterday's carrier the wrong one today. When that happens, we re-shop your coverage without you having to find a new agent, refill out an application from scratch, or start a relationship over.

We handle personal insurance, farm and agribusiness, commercial accounts, life insurance and Medicare — which means one office can cover the house, the pickup, the shop, the ground you farm and the policy that protects your family if you are not here.
